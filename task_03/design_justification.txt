Data Warehouse Design Justification

The DIM_COUNTRY, DIM_TIME, and DIM_SECTOR dimensions use integer surrogate
keys (country_key, time_key, and sector_key) that are independent of source
codes and descriptions. Natural keys are retained as identifying attributes
where applicable; for example, iso3_code identifies a country and year
identifies a year.

The three fact tables represent different business processes and grains:

- FACT_ECONOMY: one row per country and year. It stores economic indicators
  such as GDP, GDP per capita, and international trade.
- FACT_SOCIETY: one row per country and year. It stores social and demographic
  indicators such as population, GNI per capita, life expectancy, and
  urbanization.
- FACT_SECTOR: one row per country, year, and sector. It stores measures of
  economic structure and employment by sector.

FACT_ECONOMY and FACT_SOCIETY share a country-year grain, but they describe
different subject areas and contain different sets of measures. FACT_SECTOR
has a more detailed country-year-sector grain. Keeping the tables separate
avoids mixing processes with different grains, duplicating measures when
joining rows by sector, and creating columns that have no meaning for some
processes. The UNIQUE constraints enforce the intended grains.

DIM_SECTOR is connected only to FACT_SECTOR because this is the only fact table
whose measures are broken down by sector. GDP, population, and life expectancy
at the country-year level are not associated with a sector in this model.
Connecting those fact tables to DIM_SECTOR would add no information and could
imply a breakdown that does not exist.

Historical Events Integration

The chosen approach is a reference/bridge table, EVENTS, connected to
DIM_COUNTRY and DIM_TIME through country_key and time_key. Each row represents
an event and stores its description, category, and qualitative economic
impact. This approach allows multiple events to be associated with the same
country and year without assigning quantitative measures to events or changing
the grain of the fact tables. event_key is a surrogate key for the EVENTS
table; country_key and time_key reference the surrogate keys in the
dimensions. EVENTS is not a fourth fact table of economic indicators, and no
DIM_EVENT was created because events are used as descriptive context rather
than as an analytical dimension for the measures in this design.
